# NY 100.10 checklist ( MagiMDM school module )

Not legal advice. Confirm with your district. Source: 8 NYCRR §100.10.

## Calendar

| When | What |
|------|------|
| July 1 | Letter of intent |
| August 15 | IHIP |
| Four even dates | Quarterly reports |
| With 4th quarterly | Annual assessment |

Hours: grades 1–6 = **900**/year; 7–12 = **990**/year. Attendance equivalent ~180 days.
1 unit ≈ 6,480 minutes ≈ 108 hours.

Quarterly must include: hours this quarter; material covered per IHIP subject; grade **or** narrative; explanation if &lt;80% of planned material.

## Grade bands (legal track)

**1–6:** arithmetic, reading, spelling, writing, English, geography, US history, science, health, music, visual arts, PE; bilingual/ESL if needed. Also cover in K–8 at least once: US history, NYS history, US + NYS constitutions. K–12 ongoing: patriotism/citizenship; alcohol/drug/tobacco health; highway/bike safety; fire/arson safety.

**7–8 (cumulative):** English 2u; history/geography 2u; science 2u; math 2u; PE regular; health regular; art 0.5u; music 0.5u; practical arts regular; library skills regular.

**9–12 (cumulative legal minimum):** English 4; social studies 4 (incl. American history 1, participation in government 0.5, economics 0.5); math **2**; science **2**; art and/or music 1; health 0.5; PE 2; electives 3. ≈18.5 units.

## Capability track (college-facing, extra)

Same English/social studies/PE/health/art. Target math 3–4, lab science 3, language 2–3, dual enrollment optional. MagiMDM `courses.track` is `legal` or `capability`. Gap report = capability minus completed.

NY does not issue a homeschool Regents diploma. Transcript + letter of completion + optional SAT/dual-enroll are the college packet.

## Dual enrollment

If the child meets compulsory education via full-time college (≥12 semester hours), the IHIP names the institution and subjects. Those courses are `kind=college` in `courses`.
